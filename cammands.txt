List of tables and their key entities/fields:
1. Users
For managing system users and roles:-
- name: User name.
- email: User email (unique).
- password
- role: Role of the user (e.g., admin, manager).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


2. Products
To store information about the products in inventory:-
- name: Product name.
- sku: Stock Keeping Unit (unique identifier).
- description: Product description.
- price: Product price.
- quantity: Stock quantity.
- unit: Unit of measurement (e.g., pieces, kg).
- brand_id: Foreign key to brands table.
- category_id: Foreign key to categories table.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


3. Categories
To categorize products:-
- name: Category name.
- description: Category description (optional).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


4. Brands
For managing product brands:-
- name: Brand name.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


5. Suppliers
To manage suppliers of products:-
- name: Supplier name.
- contact_person: Contact person.
- phone: Phone number.
- email: Email address.
- address: Supplier address.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


6. Customers
To track customers for sales:-
- name: Customer name.
- email: Email address.
- phone: Phone number.
- address: Customer address.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


7. Purchases
To manage inventory stock purchases:-
- supplier_id: Foreign key to suppliers table.
- purchase_date: Date of purchase.
- total_amount: Total amount of purchase.
- status: Status of the purchase (e.g., pending, completed).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


8. Purchase_Items
To track individual items in a purchase:-
- purchase_id: Foreign key to purchases table.
- product_id: Foreign key to products table.
- quantity: Quantity purchased.
- price: Price per unit.
- total: Total price (quantity * price).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


9. Sales
To manage sales transactions:-
- customer_id: Foreign key to customers table.
- sale_date: Date of sale.
- total_amount: Total sale amount.
- status: Sale status (e.g., pending, completed).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


10. Sale_Items
To track individual items sold in a transaction:-
- sale_id: Foreign key to sales table.
- product_id: Foreign key to products table.
- quantity: Quantity sold.
- price: Price per unit.
- total: Total price (quantity * price).
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


11. Stock_Adjustments
To record manual stock adjustments:-
- product_id: Foreign key to products table.
- adjustment_type: Type of adjustment (e.g., addition, subtraction).
- quantity: Quantity adjusted.
- reason: Reason for adjustment.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


12. Inventories
To track current stock levels:-
- product_id: Foreign key to products table.
- quantity: Current quantity in stock.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


13. Warehouses
For multi-warehouse support:-
- name: Warehouse name.
- location: Warehouse location.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps


14. Stock_Transfers
To manage stock transfers between warehouses:-
- from_warehouse_id: Source warehouse (foreign key).
- to_warehouse_id: Destination warehouse (foreign key).
- product_id: Foreign key to products table.
- quantity: Quantity transferred.
- transfer_date: Date of transfer.
- created_at/updated_at: Timestamps
